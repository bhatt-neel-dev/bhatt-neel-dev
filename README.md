### About

I'm an AI engineer at Motadata in Ahmedabad, India. Most of my work is building multi-agent systems for enterprise teams, and the plumbing around them: MCP, retrieval, tracing, and log pipelines that keep running under real load.

In my own time I build **[APILens](https://apilens.ai)**, an open-source observability platform, and I contribute to **[Agno](https://github.com/agno-agi/agno)**, the agent framework I use every day.

What I care about: systems that are easy to reason about, numbers you can check, and agents you can trust with real work.

- **Nano Banana toolkit** ([#5422](https://github.com/agno-agi/agno/pull/5422)): image generation for Agno agents, released in [v2.3.1](https://github.com/agno-agi/agno/releases/tag/v2.3.1).
